# Code of conduct

Sideline is built first for people who are usually left out of watching sport. The project should treat its contributors
the same way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source, to MCP or to the codebase.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Make room for people who use assistive technology: describe screenshots in words, and do not assume everyone can see
  a diagram.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and
sexualised language or imagery.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/NelsonFodjo/sideline/security/advisories/new) (it reaches the maintainer
only; use it for conduct reports too) or by a direct message on GitHub to [@NelsonFodjo](https://github.com/NelsonFodjo).
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues, and ban contributors, who break this
code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
