Sideline: the demo video, from idea to upload

What to say, what to show, how to record it, and what never to claim. Target 2:45, hard limit 3:00. Submission closes Friday 23 October 2026, 12:00 pm Pacific (23:00 in Mauritius).
The video in one sentence. Anyone who wants a match now, without opening an app, searching or typing, just asks, and a server that remembers what they have heard answers in a few seconds: football, basketball, American football, hockey or baseball.

1. The problem, said so that everyone sees themselves in it

The first twenty seconds decide whether the judge cares. Do not open with "blind people". Open with a moment everybody knows, then arrive at the people who need it most. The product is built first for blind and low-vision fans; the problem is wider than that.

WhoThe momentWhat they do today
A driver, a cook, someone with their hands full"Did we score?"Stop, unlock a phone, open an app, wait for it to load, scroll.
A person in a meeting or on a train, eyes elsewhere"What's the score?"Search, tap, squint, close.
Someone who just arrived late at the match"What did I miss?"Scroll a text feed back to the start.
A blind or low-vision fanAll of it: score in a corner, line-ups on a graphic nobody reads outRely on someone beside them, or miss the match.
Narration, 0:00 to 0:20. "You want the score. So you stop what you're doing, unlock your phone, open an app, search for the match, scroll. Or you just ask. If you can't see a screen at all, asking isn't a shortcut, it's the only way in. Sideline is the friend who just tells you."

Do not say or show: a number of users, a claim of testing with blind users beyond what the team will state first-hand, a statistic about blindness or about phone use that is not in the repository, a claim that it runs on real Alexa+.

3. The live football section (0:32 to 1:20)

Everything here is spoken aloud with hands off the keyboard. Use the real match you chose on the day (make next-matches; a finished match from yesterday is fine and safest). The wording of the answers is whatever the data says that day: if one is not what you want, record that question again. 5. Under the hood: the explainer animation (1:48 to 2:16)

This is the part that shows the judges how it was built, in plain language. Generate it with docs/video/ANIMATION_PROMPT.md (paste the prompt into Claude, attach the screenshots listed there, get one HTML file, screen-record it at 1080p).

  1. Someone asks by voice.
  2. The host (Alexa+) reads the thirty tool descriptions and picks one. The quality of a description is product surface.
  3. A standard MCP call (JSON-RPC over Streamable HTTP, spec 2025-11-25) reaches our server.
  4. Inside the server: listener memory (DynamoDB), the live feed (cached only seconds), the spoiler guard (a clock, not a guess).
  5. The answer returns twice: content (words for the ear) and structuredContent (data for any screen).
  6. The same pipe serves every sport.
How the pieces map, if a judge thinks in layers (an analogy, not our architecture diagram): the view is whatever the person talks to (Alexa+, or our page); the controller is the set of MCP tools that decide what to do; the model is the state and the live feeds. Because the view only ever speaks MCP, the same server works with any client.

6. Recording, step by step

The day before

  1. make test (should say "all suites passed"), python3 scripts/check_with_sdk.py in a virtualenv with mcp installed.
  2. Pick the match: make next-matches. A finished match from the last 24 hours is safest. Write down both team names and two players.
  3. Free the port: lsof -iTCP:8765 -sTCP:LISTEN should print nothing, then make run and make sim.
  4. Rehearse the football questions and the montage once each, off camera. Re-word any that miss; do not change the product to suit the take.
  5. Generate the animation, play it through once, check every word against ANIMATION_PROMPT.md.

Recording day

  1. Clean browser window, no bookmarks bar, one tab, zoom 125%. Allow the microphone once. Turn off notifications, close anything with a logo.
  2. Hide .env and any terminal that prints keys. Settings: leave defaults (the wake word is off, so she answers whatever she hears; commentary on).
  3. Record the screen at 1080p, your own voice as narrator, microphone close, no music. Keep the browser's audio: her voice is part of the demo.
  4. Ask each question aloud, hands off the keyboard, and pause 2 seconds after each answer. Stay silent while she thinks: the wait is the evidence (about 3.5 to 5 seconds for a model answer, measured in docs/SPEECH.md; a plain score can skip the model and come sooner).
  5. If an answer is wrong or throttled, say nothing, stop, and retake the question. A faked take is worse than a second one. Never speed up or splice an answer.

After recording

  1. Trim to under 2:55. Add captions (auto-generate, then correct names and "Amazon Nova Pro").
  2. Check every frame for keys, e-mails, account ids, club crests, league logos. Scrub or crop.
  3. Upload public (YouTube or Vimeo). Title: Sideline: a friend for the match, in your ear.
  4. Put the link in the Devpost form and in docs/HACKATHON.md ("Demo video"). Submit before the deadline: nothing can change after.

7. Never say, never show

  • That it runs on real Alexa+, that it is hosted on AWS, or any number of users.
  • That it was tested with blind users, unless the team says so first-hand.
  • Club crests, league logos, broadcast footage, third-party music. Team names in text are fine.
  • Honours, trophies, stadium capacity or past winners as if the feed had them: it does not, and she says so.
  • Any API key, account id or e-mail address on screen.

8. If something goes wrong on camera

It happensDo this
"Sorry, an error occurred" (the model throttled)Wait 20 seconds, ask again. Keep the take; it shows the honest failure message.
She does not respondSay it again once. If nothing, check the microphone permission and the Dev log (Alt+D) for "ignored (...)".
No live matchUse a finished match; skip commentary; say "when a match is in play, it keeps me posted".
The server will not start: "already running"lsof -iTCP:8765 -sTCP:LISTEN, kill <PID>, start again, or SIDELINE_PORT=8766 make run.
