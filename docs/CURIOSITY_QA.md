# Fifty questions a football fan asks, and what Sideline answered

Asked of the real thing on **16 October 2026**: the server, Amazon Nova Pro and the live ESPN feed, by `python3 scripts/curiosity_check.py`, about **Lyon 3-2 Crystal Palace** (UEFA Europa League, 15 October 2026, Groupama Stadium, Lyon). The answers are what came back, cut at 260 characters; nothing was edited. The verdicts are ours, from reading each answer against the feed.

**All 174 questions on this page** (these 50, 24 across the other sports, and 100 in round 2): **129 good, 21 honest 'not in the feed' (or she asks which game), 9 partly right, 15 wrong.**

**Result: 34 good, 6 honestly 'not in the feed', 4 partly right, 6 wrong.** The first full run, before the fixes below, had 23 of 50 weak, off-topic or a data dump (from that first run, whose answers are not kept here).

**A harness problem we have not proven.** Questions 18, 19, 27 and 28 got answers that fit a neighbouring question (18 got the Tolisso profile asked for at 16, 19 got Nakamura, asked about at 18; 27 and 28 got Crystal Palace's next match). It looks as if the answers were shifted against the questions in this run. That would be a fault in the test harness, not in the tools, but it is not proven, so all four are counted as wrong.

**How the questions were fixed, in the tools and not in the page** (a real Alexa+ would only see the MCP): player questions (`get_player_profile`), 'who assisted Pino's goal' (new `player` argument on `get_event_detail`), one number from the stats (new `stat` argument on `get_match_stats`), 'was there a penalty', 'what formation', 'who is injured', 'who is top of the table' all got phrasings in their descriptions and a sentence on what the neighbouring tools are *not* for. Three guards in the simulator's agent: rules and meanings (offside, VAR) are answered with no tools; honours, stadium size, 'the best in the world' and past winners are declined plainly; and a model that tries to call a tool it was not given is not read aloud. Two bugs outside the tools: 'last year' was heard as the team name 'Bayer', and a page opened with a match left over from an earlier session.

## Round 2: 100 more across five sports

Asked on 16 October 2026 by `python3 scripts/curiosity_check.py --set round2`, each question as its **own listener** so one answer cannot colour the next. Basketball, American football, hockey, baseball and football, 20 each, about real games of 15 and 16 October. The text is what came back, cut at 150 characters.

**Result after the fixes: 83 good, 13 honest (not in the feed, or she asks which game), 3 partly right, 1 wrong.** The first clean run had 68 good, 16 honest, 8 partly and 8 wrong. Three answers (28, 79, 81) were re-asked on their own after the last fix; the other 97 are from one full run.

**Fixed on the way, each with a test:**
- A false "Did you mean...?" that blocked about 15 of the first 100 (Seahawks became Hawks, Mahomes became Partick Thistle, Brunson became Bruins, Flames became Fulham); first names and plain words rewritten as teams ("Auston" to "Aston", "triple-double" to "Tripoli").
- Basketball baskets with no team, so a Raptors player was put on the Knicks.
- Players, season lines, squads and team search that only worked for football: now basketball, American football, hockey and baseball too, including nicknames ("Oilers", "Chiefs", "Bo Nix").
- Hockey and American football team stats, a basketball record read as "428 goals", a feed code ("eng.w.1") read out as a league name.
- **New:** league leaders for the NBA, NFL, NHL and MLB; one player's game from the box score ("how many points did Brunson score last night"); who pitched; the playoff series score; one number from the match statistics ("fouls", "rushing yards", "power plays").

**A test that fooled us once:** a server left over from an earlier run kept answering with old code, so some results did not show our fixes. The check script now refuses to start if its port is taken.

**What the honest ones show:** the feed has no play-by-play for NFL games, no baseball events or team hit counts, no half-time scores and no injury report for some teams. She says so.
